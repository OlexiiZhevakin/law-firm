## 1. Licensing of financial institutions
`/en/services/licensing`

**Meta title:** Financial institution licensing — regulatory counsel | HARLIB
**Meta description:** Authorisation of lending, leasing, factoring, payment, e-money and insurance businesses: perimeter analysis, ownership structure, capital, application file, regulator liaison.

**H1:** Licensing of financial institutions

We take financial services businesses through authorisation — from establishing whether a licence is required to the regulator's decision.

### Perimeter and structuring
- Perimeter analysis: whether the proposed model amounts to a regulated financial service and which permissions it requires
- Choice of licence type and scope of permitted activities
- Ownership structuring to meet transparency requirements down to ultimate beneficial owners
- Capital plan and evidence of the source of funds contributed by each shareholder
- Incorporation of the applicant entity or pre-application restructuring of an existing one

### Lending, leasing, factoring and guarantees
- Scope of permissions across the intended product range
- Internal governance documents: risk management, internal control, AML/CFT, consumer protection, outsourcing
- Business plan — structure, description of the business and alignment with the regulator's expectations
- Assembly and submission of the full application file
- Responses to the regulator's queries and revisions to the file
- Extension of the licence to additional services; removal of a service from the scope

### Payment services and e-money
- Determining the appropriate status: payment institution, small payment institution, e-money institution, technical service provider
- Capital requirements applicable to the intended combination of services
- Safeguarding arrangements for client funds
- Documentation on operational and security risk management, authentication, information security and third-party service providers
- User-facing contractual documentation and pre-contractual disclosure
- Application for authorisation or registration, and support through the review

### Insurance
- Selection of classes of insurance and scope of permissions, including inbound reinsurance
- Capital requirements applicable to the intended scope
- Evidence of the source of funds contributed by shareholders
- Policy wordings by class, standard contracts and pre-contractual disclosure
- Governance system and allocation of key functions
- Assembly of the application file and support through the review
- Extension of the licence to additional classes for authorised insurers

### Where we work
- **Ukraine** — full-scope representation before the National Bank of Ukraine
- **EU** — jurisdiction selection and authorisation with passporting across member states
- **United Kingdom** — authorisation and variations of permission
- **Asia** — Singapore, Hong Kong and the UAE financial centres, in coordination with local counsel where local admission is required

## 2. Acquiring a licensed financial institution
`/en/services/acquisitions`

**Meta title:** Acquiring a licensed bank, insurer or payment company | HARLIB
**Meta description:** Support on the acquisition of an authorised financial institution: regulatory and financial due diligence, deal structuring, change-of-control approval, post-closing integration.

**H1:** Acquiring a licensed financial institution

For most investors, entering a market by acquiring an authorised entity is faster and more predictable than a de novo application. We advise on acquisitions of banks, insurers, payment institutions and finance companies.

### Target search and initial assessment
- Target criteria matched to the investor's objective
- Preliminary assessment of available targets by scope of licence, state of governance and supervisory history
- Assessment of whether the specific acquirer is likely to be approved, before negotiations begin

### Regulatory and financial due diligence
- Scope and validity of the licence, and whether permissions cover the business as actually conducted
- History of supervisory measures, directions, restrictions and open findings
- State of the governance system and internal policies
- Ownership structure and completeness of beneficial ownership disclosure
- Directors and key function holders, and the status of their approvals
- Capital position and compliance with prudential requirements
- Asset quality, liability structure and financial reporting metrics
- Contract portfolio, litigation and customer claims
- AML/CFT and consumer protection compliance
- Report setting out the risks identified, their effect on price, and the conditions worth closing before signing

### Deal structuring and completion
- Acquisition structure: direct or indirect acquisition, intermediate entities, staged entry
- Sale and purchase agreement: conditions precedent tied to the regulator's decision, warranties and indemnities, price adjustment mechanics
- Change-of-control approval — [see the dedicated page](/en/services/qualifying-holding)
- Approval of incoming directors and key function holders
- Coordination with the seller, auditors and other deal advisers

### Post-closing
- Remediation plan addressing the findings from due diligence
- Updating internal policies and the governance structure for the new owner
- Extending or varying permissions for the new business model
- Support on the first engagement with the regulator after the change of control

### Where we work
- **Ukraine** — full-scope representation before the National Bank of Ukraine
- **EU** — change-of-control approvals for credit institutions, insurers and investment firms, in coordination with local counsel
- **United Kingdom** — change in control notifications and assessment
- **Asia** — controller approvals in Singapore, Hong Kong and the UAE financial centres

## 3. Crypto-asset service provider licensing
`/en/services/crypto`

**Meta title:** Crypto-asset service provider licensing | HARLIB
**Meta description:** Authorisation of crypto businesses: jurisdiction selection, application files, custody and governance documentation, transition from registration regimes to full authorisation.

**H1:** Crypto-asset service provider licensing

We help crypto businesses choose the right jurisdiction and obtain authorisation — and prepare those already operating for regimes that are about to bite.

### Jurisdiction selection
- Mapping target markets against the regimes that will govern them
- Comparison of authorisation routes by scope, timing and substance requirements
- Assessment of whether the current model falls inside or outside the perimeter, including non-custodial arrangements
- Group structuring across licensed and unlicensed entities

### Authorisation
- Definition of the services to be covered: custody and administration, operating a trading platform, exchange, execution and reception and transmission of orders, placement, advice, portfolio management, transfer services
- Ownership structure and capital documentation
- Internal policies: safeguarding of client assets, conflicts of interest, complaints handling, operational resilience, AML/CFT
- Custody documentation: trust arrangements, private-key controls, reconciliation procedures
- Disclosure documentation for token issuers
- Application file and support through the review

### Transition and remediation
- Moving from an AML-registration regime to full authorisation
- Migrating from national regimes to a harmonised one
- Gap analysis against a regime that has been adopted but is not yet in application

### Where we work
- **EU** — authorisation of crypto-asset service providers with passporting across member states; issuer regimes for asset-referenced and e-money tokens
- **United Kingdom** — authorisation under the cryptoasset regime, including the transitional route for firms currently registered for AML purposes
- **Asia** — Singapore, Hong Kong and the UAE financial centres

## 4. Qualifying holding and change-of-control approvals
`/en/services/qualifying-holding`

**Meta title:** Qualifying holding and change of control approvals | HARLIB
**Meta description:** Preparing acquirers of qualifying holdings in banks, insurers and financial companies: reputation, financial standing, source of funds, ownership transparency.

**H1:** Qualifying holding and change-of-control approvals

We prepare acquirers for the regulator's assessment and run the approval process through to decision.

### What we do
- Establish whether an approval obligation arises, including indirect acquisitions and significant influence without formal shareholding
- Screen the acquirer against the regulator's criteria before any documents are prepared
- Assess the reputation of the acquirer and connected persons and identify grounds for refusal
- Prepare evidence of the acquirer's financial standing
- Reconstruct and document the chain of origin of the acquisition funds
- Disclose the ownership chain down to ultimate beneficial owners and resolve gaps and nominee arrangements
- Structure the transaction: acquisition vehicle, intermediate entities, conditions precedent in the sale agreement
- Coordinate with auditors on reports submitted to the regulator
- Handle foreign documents: translation, legalisation, apostille, certificates from foreign registers
- Manage the review and respond to the regulator's queries

### Typical situations
- Acquisition of a bank, insurer or financial company
- Group reorganisation changing indirect holdings
- Share issue taking an existing shareholder across a notification threshold
- Change in ultimate beneficial owners, succession, family structuring
- Exit of a shareholder whose participation creates a regulatory risk

### Where we work
- **Ukraine** — full-scope representation before the National Bank of Ukraine
- **EU** — qualifying holding assessments for credit institutions, insurers and investment firms, in coordination with local counsel
- **United Kingdom** — change in control notifications and assessment
- **Asia** — controller approvals in Singapore, Hong Kong and the UAE financial centres

## 5. Capital increases and subordinated debt
`/en/services/capital`

**Meta title:** Capital increases and subordinated debt approvals | HARLIB
**Meta description:** Regulatory support for increases of share capital and subordinated debt: source of funds, terms that qualify as capital, approval by the regulator.

**H1:** Capital increases and subordinated debt

We support recapitalisation of financial institutions — through shareholder contributions or subordinated debt.

### Increase of share capital
- Timing the recapitalisation against regulatory deadlines
- Documenting the source of funds contributed by each shareholder
- Assessing the effect on the ownership structure and triggering a change-of-control filing where a threshold is crossed
- Corporate resolutions, amendments to constitutional documents, registration filings
- Coordination with auditors
- Support through the regulator's review

### Subordinated debt
- Structuring the facility: tenor, pricing, ranking on insolvency
- Drafting or revising the agreement so that the proceeds qualify as capital
- Removing terms that disqualify the instrument: early repayment without approval, security in favour of the lender, set-off rights
- Verification of the lender's source of funds
- Support through approval and inclusion of the proceeds in capital
- Review of subsequent amendments so that the instrument retains its status

### Where we work
- **Ukraine** — full-scope representation before the National Bank of Ukraine
- **EU** — classification of capital instruments under the prudential frameworks for banks and insurers
- **United Kingdom** — capital instruments under the domestic prudential regime
- **Asia** — Singapore, Hong Kong and the UAE financial centres

## Editorial notes

- **No statutory citations.** Rules change; the copy should not. Where a specific figure or deadline is genuinely useful for conversion — such as an authorisation window with a hard cut-off — put it in an Insights article and link from the service page, so a single article needs updating rather than the whole site.
- **"In coordination with local counsel"** appears in the jurisdiction blocks. Adjust or drop it per jurisdiction once the referral network is settled.
- **Ukraine is absent from the crypto page** by design — there is no operative authorisation regime to advise on yet. Add it once one is in force.
- **hreflang.** URL slugs differ from the Ukrainian ones deliberately, since English readers search different terms — set reciprocal pairs manually rather than deriving them from the slug. The crypto page has no Ukrainian counterpart: give it `en` and `x-default` only.
